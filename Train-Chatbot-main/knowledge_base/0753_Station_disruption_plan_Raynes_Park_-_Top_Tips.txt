Station disruption plan for Raynes Park. Category: station disruption. Location: Raynes Park. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): This station is not accessible by wheelchair users on the ‘down’ side (platform 3 & 4) Passengers should change at Motspur park or New Malden if they have arrived on the down side in error During the morning significant passenger numbers can build up on the London-bound side. A queue can be put in place on the ramp and passengers redirected to get a bus to Wimbledon to change.
