Station disruption plan for Richmond. Category: station disruption. Location: Richmond. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Disruption on District Line Richmond branch . East Putney District Line branch is a 6 minute walk from Putney Rail station. Disruption on London Overground Richmond branch . Shepherds Bush London Overground branch depart from Platform 1 at Clapham Junction . Disruption on SWR services towards London Waterloo. London Waterloo – District Line change at Westminster, or London Overground change at Willesden Junction. Vauxhall – District Line change at London Victoria. Clapham Junction – London Overground change at Willesden Junction. Hampton Court Palace. Quicker to travel via local bus than via train.
