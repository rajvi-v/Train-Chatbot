Station disruption plan for Twickenham. Category: station disruption. Location: Twickenham. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Disruption on District Line Richmond branch . East Putney District Line branch is a 6 minute walk from Putney Rail station. Disruption on London Overground Richmond branch . Shepherds Bush London Overground branch depart from Platform 1 at Clapham Junction . Disruption on SWR services towards London Waterloo. London Waterloo – District Line from Richmond change at Westminster, or London Overground change at Willesden Junction. Vauxhall – District Line from Richmond change at London Victoria. Clapham Junction – London Overground from Richmond change at Willesden Junction. Trains towards London During disruption, it may be quicker to avoid going via Richmond to get to London. Trains go from platform 5 via Kingston and Hounslow – these are advertised as Wimbledon and Chiswick on the CIS screens
