Station disruption plan for Axminster. Category: station disruption. Location: Axminster. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Limited Taxi rank Local taxi firms listed on the ‘Onward Journey’ Information Poster located at side entrance to station Replacement buses Car park needs to be coned off day before the use of replacement buses. – Buses have difficulty reaching the station during severe weather conditions due to using minor roads and country lanes. Terminating trains Axminster is suitable for terminating trains due to ease of access for buses.
