Station disruption plan for Ashurst New Forest. Category: station disruption. Location: Ashurst New Forest. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Ashurst can become isolated and during times of disruption can be used as a ‘not to stop’ order to support recovery Any passengers travelling down the line are advised to make their way to Brockenhurst and any passengers travelling up the line are advised to make their way to Southampton central Options in terms of alternative transport are very limited at Ashurst new forest with a limited bus service ( 1 per hour in both directions), very limited taxi service and no alternative rail options The message to passengers may well need to be one of patience. There is a bus option (although acceptance unlikely) but ultimately they may well be best waiting for whatever the next rail service is Any disruptive period further up or down the line may have a delayed impact on Ashurst. Trains may continue to run for a period of time Take the opportunity to plan ahead as any gap between trains is likely to be extended; sometimes 2-3 hour
