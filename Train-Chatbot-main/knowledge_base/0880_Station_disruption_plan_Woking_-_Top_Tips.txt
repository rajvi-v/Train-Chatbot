Station disruption plan for Woking. Category: station disruption. Location: Woking. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): If no road replacement transport is available and if practical obtain taxis If cab-line are unable to provided taxis in a reasonable time, use taxis off of Woking Rank. Large gaps in train services . Contact SCP to contact wicc to request stop orders issued on alternative services. If disruption on the Alton line. Passengers can be advised to travel to Farnborough Main and change for lo c al bus service to Aldershot.
