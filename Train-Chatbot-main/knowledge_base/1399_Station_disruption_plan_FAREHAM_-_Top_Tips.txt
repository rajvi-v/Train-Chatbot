Station disruption plan for FAREHAM. Category: station disruption. Location: FAREHAM. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Car park during bus replacement If bus replacement in place, need to ensure that gravel yard end of the car park is kept clear in order for buses to be able to turn. Barriers available. Nearest cash machine is outside Lidl store in West St, approx. 500 metres away
