Station disruption plan for Oxshott. Category: station disruption. Location: Oxshott. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): No services due to disruption. Customers advised to use the 513 bus to Esher, or drive to Esher or Leatherhead for Southern services. Cabline to be used for customers requiring assistance during staffed hours. Oxshott Station Cars could be considered during severe disruption if Cabline cannot provide taxis.
