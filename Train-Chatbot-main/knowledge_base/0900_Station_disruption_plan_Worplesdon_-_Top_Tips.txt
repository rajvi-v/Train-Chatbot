Station disruption plan for Worplesdon. Category: station disruption. Location: Worplesdon. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Service disruption leading to no services through Worplesdon Station. Advise passengers to use Woking or Guildford stations for alternative services (requesting car park easements at both stations).
