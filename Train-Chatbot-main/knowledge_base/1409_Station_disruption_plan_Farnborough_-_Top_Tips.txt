Station disruption plan for Farnborough. Category: station disruption. Location: Farnborough. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): If no London Bound trains operating, Customers can change at Basingstoke for GWR/Cross country trains to Reading for GWR connections to London Paddington. If no country-bound stopping services at Farnborough for a long period WICC to be contacted to request additional stops on country bound fast services for Fleet, Winchfield and Hook. Service plan for West Of England routes to terminate and start at Basingstoke. WICC to be contacted to request for !WXX to stop additional at Woking on the down direction to pick up customers for the WOE route. Crowding and become an issue on all platforms. Implement crowd management plan if needed. If too many train terminating at Woking at the period of time, this can cause overcrowding on the platforms Contact WICC to make arrangement no to terminated trains, until platform is safe and clear with footfall.
