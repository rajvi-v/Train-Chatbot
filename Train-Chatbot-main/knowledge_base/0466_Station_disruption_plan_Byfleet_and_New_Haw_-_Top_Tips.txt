Station disruption plan for Byfleet and New Haw. Category: station disruption. Location: Byfleet and New Haw. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Service disruption on mainline leading to no mainline services. Request WICC to add extra stop to Basingstoke stopping services where possible (especially during AM peak) Passengers arriving at station wanting to visit Brooklands Museum or Mercedes Benz World Advise passengers to take 436 bus directly to attractions or walk along Byfleet Road turning left out of station entrance (15-20 min walk)
