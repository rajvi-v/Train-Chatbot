Plan 8.1 Winchester - Eastleigh Bournemouth Main Line. Category: line blockage. Location: Winchester - Eastleigh Bournemouth Main Line. Blockage type: Both lines blocked. Audience: service_controller. Recommended action (service_alteration): 1Wxx xx.05 Waterloo - Weymouth & return services run Bournemouth to Weymouth . 1Bxx xx.35 Waterloo - Poole & return services Divert via Havant . 2Bxx xx.50 Winchester - Bournemouth & return services run between S outhampton and Bournemouth . 1Txx xx.09 Waterloo - Portsmouth Harbour (EH) & return services run between Eastleigh & Portsmouth . CrossCountry : 1Oxx (Down) & 1Mxx (Up) services divert between Basingstoke and Southampton Central via Laverstock/Chandlers Ford/Test Valley or services terminate/start at Reading. Shuttle rail service Basingstoke – Winchester if stock and crew available (Use stock ex 1Wxx) Trains trapped London side of blockage Terminate at Basingstoke or Winchester. Berth Barton Mill until blockage cleared then run ECS to Destination. Freight services diverted via Andover or Westbury. Bus service Winchester - Eastleigh/Southampton. Shawford served by minibus.
