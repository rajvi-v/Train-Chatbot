Station disruption plan for Bournemouth. Category: station disruption. Location: Bournemouth. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Any disruptive event further up the line will have a delayed impact on Bournemouth. Trains may continue to run for a period of time. Take the opportunity to plan ahead as any gap between trains is likely to be extended; sometimes 2-3 hours. Almost every train that stops at Bournemouth has a crew change, This leads to numerous additional delays owing to a lack of traincrew. Where possible try to liaise with the DRM team to establish those trains most at risk. If necessary it may be that someone (possibly a Traincrew Manager) needs to be based in the Messroom to support this.
