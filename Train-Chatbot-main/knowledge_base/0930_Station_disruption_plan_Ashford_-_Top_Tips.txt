Station disruption plan for Ashford. Category: station disruption. Location: Ashford. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Bus stop location Buses depart from Woodthorpe Road on the down side of the station. Some additional buses depart from Church Road – check local signage for more information. Oyster fare zones Ashford is not within the TFL zones however some buses operated by TFL pass through the area. Oyster cards are accepted on these services. Please check before boarding.
