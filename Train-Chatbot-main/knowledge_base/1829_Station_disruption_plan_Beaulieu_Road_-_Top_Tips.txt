Station disruption plan for Beaulieu Road. Category: station disruption. Location: Beaulieu Road. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Beaulieu road can become isolated at times of disruption and can be used as a ‘not to stop’ order in order to support recovery Any passengers travelling down the line are advised to make their way to Brockenhurst and any passengers travelling up the line are advised to make their way to Southampton central Options in terms of alternative transport are very limited at Beaulieu road with no bus network and very limited taxis. Also no alternative rail options The message to passengers may well need to be one of patience. There is no bus option so ultimately they may well be best waiting for next rail service Any disruptive period further up or down the line may have a delayed impact on Beaulieu road. Trains may continue to run for a period of time Take the opportunity to plan ahead as any gap between trains is likely to be extended; sometimes 2-3 hour Very limited rail service Monday– Saturday with often 2-3 hours in Advise passengers due to a lack of public transport and rail services at Beaulieu road that if possible they use their own vehicles to make their way to either Brockenhurst or Southampton central if they really need to travel
