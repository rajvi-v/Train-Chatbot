Station disruption plan for Bentley. Category: station disruption. Location: Bentley. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Service disruption leading to no services through Bentley Station. Advise passengers where possible to use Aldershot or Guildford stations for alternative services (requesting car park easements at both stations).
