Station disruption plan for Clandon. Category: station disruption. Location: Clandon. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): No trains due to disruption Customers advised to use the local bus to Guildford or Woking. This bus is very limited and therefore the use of taxis via Cabline may be more appropriate.
