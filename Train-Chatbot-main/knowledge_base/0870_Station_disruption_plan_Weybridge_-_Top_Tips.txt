Station disruption plan for Weybridge. Category: station disruption. Location: Weybridge. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Severe disruption leading to no trains on mainline Direct passengers to use branch line service changing at Virginia Water for faster services towards London Waterloo Passengers arriving at station visiting either Brooklands Museum or Mercedes Benz World Direct passengers to use 436 buses for both attractions or 15 minute walk turning right from station approach and walking down Brooklands Road.
