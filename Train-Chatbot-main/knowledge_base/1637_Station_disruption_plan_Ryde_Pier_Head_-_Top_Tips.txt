Station disruption plan for Ryde Pier Head. Category: station disruption. Location: Ryde Pier Head. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Car park during bus replacement If bus replacement in place, need to ensure that gravel yard end of the car park is kept clear in order for buses to be able to turn. Barriers available. Nearest cash machine is in Costcutters store in West St, approx. 500 metres away
