Station disruption plan for Winchester. Category: station disruption. Location: Winchester. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Practical Operation Of The Station): Flow and overcrowding on the station to be managed by the gatelines. Potential for queueing into the entrance can be managed via the downside as per the crowd control plan. Mobile microphones to be used to provide information. Station is singhle manned on the platofrm however any additional spare staff can be utilesed on the opposite platform to the dispatch staff. Gatelines should be kept in operation at all times unless there is an emergency or a bi directional rail replacement resulting in no trains calling at the station . Gatelines provide an effective way of managing the flow of people in and out of stations. Gatelines also provide a dedicated person at the entrance to the station to advise of any issues with the train service so customers can make alternative arrangements if necessary. If the gate does need to be opened it must be recorded in the gateline log. TICKET ACCEPTANCE When services are severely disrupted we can sometimes arrange for passengers to use an alternative route to complete their journey. Where this involves the use of other train/bus operators SWR will obtain agreement from them to allow customers to use these routes at no extra cost. This can also include travel arrangements for the following day if appropriate. The majority of these agreements come into place automatically when we declare CSL2 status for an incident; agreements with Cross Country, local bus operators and London Buses are made by agreement between our control room and theirs only. Alternative routes that are agreed will be communicated via the information team through emails, phone calls or our information systems during each incident. In addition, there may be easements agreed for car parking arrangements which will be agreed and communicated at either the same time or shortly after.
