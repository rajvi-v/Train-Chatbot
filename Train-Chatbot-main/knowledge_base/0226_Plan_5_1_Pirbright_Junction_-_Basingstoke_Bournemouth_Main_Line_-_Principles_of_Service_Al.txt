Plan 5.1 Pirbright Junction - Basingstoke Bournemouth Main Line. Category: line blockage. Location: Pirbright Junction - Basingstoke Bournemouth Main Line. Blockage type: DMFL or UMFL blocked. Audience: service_controller. Recommended action (service_alteration): Trains diverted via Slow lines over shortest distance. Consideration must be given to the withdrawal of station stops in order to prevent delay to Fast trains. Passengers from stations not served either circulate via adjacent stations, or by bus service, if instituted.
