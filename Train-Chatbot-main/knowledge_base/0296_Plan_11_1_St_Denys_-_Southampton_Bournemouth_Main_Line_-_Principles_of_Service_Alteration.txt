Plan 11.1 St. Denys - Southampton Bournemouth Main Line. Category: line blockage. Location: St. Denys - Southampton Bournemouth Main Line. Blockage type: All lines blocked or both lines Status: blocked in one direction (No access to Southampton station and no access to Northam Depot). Audience: service_controller. Recommended action (service_alteration): 1Wxx xx.05 Waterloo - Weymouth & return services run between Brockenhurst & Weymouth . 1Bxx xx.35 Waterloo - Poole & return services start/terminate Southampton Airport Parkway or Eastleigh . 2Bxx xx.49 Winchester - Bournemouth & return services run between Southampton Central and Bournemouth 1Txx xx.09 Waterloo - Portsmouth Harbour (EH) & return services run as booked . 2Rxx xx 48 Salisbury - Romsey and return services run only between Southampton & Salisbury via Test Valley . 2Exx xx36 Portsmouth - Southampton & return services run between Portsmouth & St. Denys (if shunt move available), Portsmouth & Woolston (if shunt move not available, & NR MOM is available). If neither shunt move nor NR MOM is available, services cancelled. GTR: 1Jxx services to Southampton Central divert and terminate / start at Fratton. GTR: 1Nxx services terminate/start at Barnham with stock and crew to step up by 2 circuits. From Southampton Central, any stock/crew to berth at Southampton Central Down Bay. (SWR Pilot Driver required for berth at SOU). GWR: Services to divert via Chandlers Ford / Botley if crew knowledge permits or terminate / start at Southampton Central. If no access to Southampton Central and the route via Chandlers Ford is unavailable then GWR to terminate at Salisbury. Control discuss with GWR crew requirements. CrossCountry : 1Oxx (Down) & 1Mxx (Up) services divert between Basingstoke and Southampton Central via Laverstock/Test Valley or services terminate/start reading . Bus service Southampton Pkwy - Bournemouth (fast) & Southampton Pkwy - Brockenhurst (all stations). If trains unable to reverse at St. Denys, Bus service from Fareham.
