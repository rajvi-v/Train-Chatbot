Station disruption plan for COSHAM. Category: station disruption. Location: COSHAM. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Disruption / engineering works Buses are not permitted to stop outside of station and stop and turn at the interchange. Turn left out of station and across level crossing and interchange is on the right.
