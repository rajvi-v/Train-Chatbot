Station disruption plan for COBHAM & STOKE D’ABERNON. Category: station disruption. Location: COBHAM & STOKE D’ABERNON. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): No train services due to disruption Customers advised to use bus/car to Weybridge, and also car to Leatherhead. Cabline should be used for customers requiring assistance during staffed hours. Cobham Cars (Taxis) could be considered if Cabline unable to provide taxis.
