Station disruption plan for Walton on Thames. Category: station disruption. Location: Walton on Thames. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Severe disruption leading to no trains on Woking main line Direct passengers to Shepperton Station fo r alternative route to Waterloo , using 555 buses or 15 minute walk. Severe disruption leading to no Woking stopping services Request WICC for extra stop on Alton up line services to bolster capacity (especially during AM peak)
