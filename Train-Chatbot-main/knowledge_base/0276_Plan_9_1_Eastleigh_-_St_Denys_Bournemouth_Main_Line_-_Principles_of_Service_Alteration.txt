Recommended action (service_alteration): 1Wxx xx.05 Waterloo - Weymouth & return services Runs Bournemouth to Weymouth . 1Bxx xx.35 Waterloo - Poole & return services Divert via Havant . 2Bxx xx.49 Winchester - Bournemouth & return services run between Southampton and Bournemouth 1Txx xx.09 Waterloo - Portsmouth Harbour (EH) & return services divert via Guildford if crew have route knowledge. . 2Rxx xx 56 Salisbury - Romsey & return services run only between Southampton & Salisbury via Test Valley . CrossCountry : 1Oxx (Down) & 1Mxx (Up) services divert between Basingstoke and Southampton Central via Laverstock/Test Valley or services terminate/start Reading. Fast bus service Basingstoke - Southampton Parkway/Southampton. SWR bus service Eastleigh - Fareham. Shawford served by minibus. Minibus Swaythling - Eastleigh - Chandlers Ford - Romsey.
