Plan 12.1 St. Denys - Southampton Bournemouth Main Line. Category: line blockage. Location: St. Denys - Southampton Bournemouth Main Line. Blockage type: All lines blocked or both lines Status: blocked in one direction (Access to Southampton station but no access to Northam Depot). Audience: service_controller. Recommended action (service_alteration): 1Wxx xx.05 Waterloo - Weymouth & return services Cancelled From Waterloo. run between Southampton & Weymouth . (SSO Branksome & Parkstone) 1Bxx xx.35 Waterloo - Poole & return run between Waterloo & Southampton Airport Parkway . (SSO Shawford ) 2Bxx xx.49 Winchester - Bournemouth & return services run Southampton Central to Bournemouth only 1Txx xx.09 Waterloo - Portsmouth Harbour (EH) & return services run as booked . 2Rxx xx 48 Salisbury - Romsey and return services run only between Southampton & Salisbury via Test Valley . 2Exx xx. Portsmouth - Southampton & return services run between Portsmouth & St. Denys (if shunt move available), Portsmouth and Woolston (if shunt move not available, & NR MOM is available). If neither shunt move nor NR MOM is available, services cancelled . GTR: 1Jxx services to Southampton Central divert and terminate / start at Fratton. GTR: 1Nxx services terminate/start at Barnham with stock and crew to step up by 2 circuits. From Southampton Central, any stock/crew to berth at Southampton Central Down Bay. (SWR Pilot Driver required for berth at SOU). GWR: Services to divert via Chandlers Ford / Botley if crew knowledge permits or terminate / start at Southampton Central. Control discuss with GWR crew requirements. CrossCountry : 1Oxx (Down) & 1Mxx (Up) services divert between Basingstoke and Southampton Central via Laverstock/Test Valley or services terminate/start Reading. Freight services divert via Andover. Bus services Eastleigh - Southampton (fast and all stations). If trains unable to reverse at St. Denys, bus service from termination point of Portsmouth services to/from Southampton.
