Station disruption plan for Ash Vale. Category: station disruption. Location: Ash Vale. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Service disruption leading to no services through Ash Vale Station. Advise passengers where possible to use North Camp Station (GWR) and change at Guildford for connecting SWR services.
