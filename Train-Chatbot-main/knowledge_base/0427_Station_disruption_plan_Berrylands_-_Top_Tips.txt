Station disruption plan for Berrylands. Category: station disruption. Location: Berrylands. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): If Hampton Court branch line is not operating Control will try to organise a replacement bus service – this will run from the Victoria Road forecourt Ensure someone is allocated as bus liaison. Taxis can usually continue to operate from the rank If several branch or mainline bus replacements are organised, stopping services operate from the Victoria Road forecourt and fast (non-stop) services to Clapham from the downside forecourt. In this situation the front forecourt will need to be closed off to taxis and other vehicles and resources will need to be found to cover both traffic control and bus liaison. It will be necessary to ensure buses have room to manoeuvre on the downside and to resource bus liaison staff. If taxis are authorised this should be through Cabline in the first instance. If Cabline are unable to provide in reasonable time customers may be issue vouchers for black cabs There will be a need to allocate resources to issue vouchers and assisting customers. Station Cars are part of Cabline and may assist – they are located on the downside. If needs be refreshment vouchers can be issued to delayed customers using the Back on Track Vouchers Pads are available in the Ticket Office, the station supervisor or any manager. Caf f e Nero redeem refreshment vouchers from either of the two outlets on the station
