Station disruption plan for Fleet. Category: station disruption. Location: Fleet. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): If no London Bound trains operating, Customers can change at Basingstoke for GWR/Cross country trains to Reading for GWR connections to London Paddington. If no country Bound stopping services for a long period. WICC to be contacted to request additional stops on country bound fast services.
