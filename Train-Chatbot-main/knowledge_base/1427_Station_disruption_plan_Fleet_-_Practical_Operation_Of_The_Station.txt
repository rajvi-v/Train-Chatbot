Station disruption plan for Fleet. Category: station disruption. Location: Fleet. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Practical Operation Of The Station): This is station is staffed with ticket office clerk on early and late shifts , Monday to Saturday and one shift on Sundays. The RO Line cleaner also attend daily , who can also assist with customer service during disruption if the line cleaner is present at the time. The ticket office staff will provide customer services duties in the booking hall. There are no gateline barriers at this station. There is no crowd management plan for this station. Fleet - station is a deemed medium risk station. Gatelines should be kept in operation at all times unless there is an emergency. Gatelines provide an effective way of managing the flow of people in and out of stations. Gatelines also provide a dedicated person at the entrance to the station to advise of any issues with the train service so customers can make alternative arrangements if necessary. If the gate does need to be opened it must be recorded in the gateline log. TICKET ACCEPTANCE When services are severely disrupted we can sometimes arrange for passengers to use an alternative route to complete their journey. Where this involves the use of other train/bus operators SWR will obtain agreement from them to allow customers to use these routes at no extra cost. This can also include travel arrangements for the following day if appropriate. The majority of these agreements come into place automatically when we declare CSL2 status for an incident; agreements with Cross Country, local bus operators and London Buses are made by agreement between our control room and theirs only. Alternative routes that are agreed will be communicated via the information team through emails, phone calls or our information systems during each incident. In addition, there may be easements agreed for car parking arrangements which will be agreed and communicated at either the same time or shortly after.
