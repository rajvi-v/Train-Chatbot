Station disruption plan for North Sheen. Category: station disruption. Location: North Sheen. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Disruption on District Line Richmond branch . East Putney District Line branch is a 6 minute walk from Putney Rail station. Disruption on London Overground Richmond branch . Shepherds Bush London Overground branch depart from Platform 1 at Clapham Junction . Disruption on SWR services towards London Waterloo. London Waterloo – District Line from Richmond change at Westminster, or London Overground change at Willesden Junction. Vauxhall – District Line from Richmond change at London Victoria. Clapham Junction – London Overground from Richmond change at Willesden Junction. Hampton Court Palace. Quicker to travel via local bus than via train. Richmond Station 15 minute walk from North Sheen, head up past Sainsburys and turn left.
