Station disruption plan for Bitterne. Category: station disruption. Location: Bitterne. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Bitterne can become isolated during times of disruption and can be used as a ‘not to stop’ order to help with recovery In general terms any passengers moving towards Portsmouth or Guildford need to make their way to Fareham and any passengers towards Weymouth or London the line need to make their way to Southampton Options in terms of alternative transport are limited with reduced bus service to local stations and very limited taxis The message to passengers may well need to be one of patience. There is limited bus service and taxis limited so ultimately it may be worth waiting for the next train service Any disruptive event further up or down the line will have a delayed impact on Bitterne. Trains may continue to run for a period of time Take the opportunity to plan ahead as any gap between trains is likely to be extended; sometimes 2-3 hours
