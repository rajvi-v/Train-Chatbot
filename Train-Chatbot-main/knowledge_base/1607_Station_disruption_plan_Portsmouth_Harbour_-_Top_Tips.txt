Station disruption plan for Portsmouth Harbour. Category: station disruption. Location: Portsmouth Harbour. Blockage type: station or platform disruption. Audience: station_staff. Recommended action (Top Tips): Station Control Point is on the main concourse near platform 5 Station RO2s Office is near the stop blocks on platform 5. Please note this is the signing in point, also Safety Notice boards and First Aid Box are located here. Station exit via platform 1 then go over footbridge to platform 1 Toilets and Booking Office are on platform 1 . Communication around the station Carry Radio’s, great help during times of disruption
